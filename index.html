OUR STORY

Rooted in Bihar,
Made for the World

Root n Raw was born from a simple belief: the best snacks come from the best ingredients, grown in the most natural way possible. Bihar's wetlands produce some of the world's finest Makhana — and we bring that purity directly to your snack bowl.

We work closely with local farmers, ensuring fair practices, sustainable harvesting, and zero compromise on quality. Every tin that reaches you is a piece of Bihar's heritage.
